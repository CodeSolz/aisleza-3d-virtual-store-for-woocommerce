# Changelog

All notable changes to Aisleza — 3D Virtual Store for WooCommerce. The release body on GitHub is taken from the matching `### Version:` heading.

### Version: 1.0.0

First release on WordPress.org.

- Walk-in 3D store built from the WooCommerce categories you choose, with hanging aisle signs.
- Products on lit oak shelves with their catalog images and price tags; free shelf space styled with decor.
- A trolley that is the real WooCommerce cart, with a drawer for quantity, remove, subtotal and checkout.
- Product panel with gallery, price, stock, quantity and variation choices.
- Checkout lanes leading to the normal WooCommerce checkout.
- Keyboard, mouse and on-screen joystick controls; auto-walk to any aisle; store map.
- Shelf wall and list views; full-window or inside-theme layouts.
- Optional WearGraph virtual try-on.
- The store's look is a skin (data only), checked before use; one built-in skin, Boutique.
