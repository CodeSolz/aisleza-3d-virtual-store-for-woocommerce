A 3D virtual store and virtual showroom for WooCommerce: customers walk aisles of your products and buy with your existing cart and checkout.

== Description ==

**Give customers a new way to explore your online store.**

Aisleza is a free 3D virtual store plugin for WooCommerce. It turns your catalog into a walk-through 3D shop, an online showroom customers explore right in the browser, with no app, headset or VR equipment needed. It brings 3D ecommerce and virtual shopping to the store you already run. Your categories become aisles, your product images appear on shelves, and customers browse, choose product options and add items to their trolley as they walk around.

The trolley is connected to your existing WooCommerce cart. When customers are ready to buy, they continue to your regular checkout, with the payment and shipping options you already offer.

You do not need 3D models or a new catalog. Choose the categories you want to show, arrange your aisles and publish the virtual store on its own page. Your regular shop stays available alongside it.

[Explore the live demo](https://aisleza.com/demo/)

### Build a 3D store from the products you already sell

* **Categories become aisles.** Choose which categories to include, set their order and customise each aisle's sign text and colour. Subcategories appear as filters within the aisle.
* **Products appear on shelves.** Shelf displays use your existing product images and show regular and sale prices on shelf-edge price tags. Decorative items fill unused shelf space, so a small catalog still looks good.
* **A furnished store interior.** The free plugin includes a boutique-style interior with oak shelving, shelf lighting, a slatted ceiling and greenery.
* **Your store name on display.** Your store name appears at the entrance and on the trolley handle.
* **A trolley connected to WooCommerce.** Customers review items, change quantities, remove products and continue to checkout from the trolley drawer.
* **Free shipping progress.** When your shipping zone offers free shipping above a minimum order amount, the trolley shows how much more the customer needs to add, with a progress bar, using the same rule as your checkout.
* **"You may also like" in the trolley.** The cross-sells you set on your products appear in the trolley. Choosing one walks the customer to its shelf.
* **Checkout near the exit.** Checkout lanes take customers to your existing WooCommerce checkout.

### Browse, choose and buy

Customers open a product straight from the shelf to see its images, price, star rating and review count, availability and short description.

* **Simple products:** choose a quantity and add the product to the trolley.
* **Variable products:** choose the available options, such as size or colour, and add the exact variation. Combinations that do not exist are disabled.
* **Other product types:** grouped, external and other special product types link to their product pages.

WooCommerce keeps managing your prices, stock, taxes, discounts and checkout. Aisleza adds a new way to browse, not a new way to sell.

### Explore on desktop, tablet or phone

* **Keyboard:** move with W, A, S, D or the arrow keys. Hold Shift to move faster and press Enter to open the product in front of you.
* **Mouse:** drag to look around, click a product to view it, or click the floor to walk to a spot.
* **Touch devices:** move through the store with an on-screen joystick.
* **Store navigation:** use the aisle buttons or the store map to go straight to an aisle.

### Alternative ways to browse

Customers can pick the view that suits them and their device.

* **Shelf wall view:** browse one aisle at a time in a lighter, flat layout. Browsers without 3D support use this view automatically.
* **List view:** browse products in a plain list. Keyboard users can reach the current aisle's product list straight after the 3D view.
* **Regular shop link:** return to your standard WooCommerce shop at any time.

### Who it's for

Aisleza suits any WooCommerce shop that wants an immersive, interactive shopping experience: fashion and clothing stores (a virtual boutique), gift and lifestyle shops, beauty, toys, books, home decor and furniture (a virtual furniture showroom), food and drink, and agencies building online stores for clients. It turns browsing into a visit, which helps shoppers discover more of your range.

### Optional virtual try-on with WearGraph

If WearGraph is installed and set up, supported products show a **Try on me** button that opens the WearGraph fitting room.

WearGraph is optional. Aisleza works on its own, and try-on buttons stay hidden when the integration is not available. The Aisleza settings page shows what the integration needs.

== Installation ==

1. Install and activate WooCommerce, then install and activate Aisleza (Plugins → Add New Plugin, search for "Aisleza").
2. Open **Aisleza** in your WordPress admin menu, below WooCommerce.
3. Under **Store page**, select **Create virtual store page** to create a draft, or select an existing page containing the **Aisleza 3D Store** block or the `[aisleza]` shortcode.
4. Under **Aisles**, select the product categories to show and set their order.
5. Under **Store**, set your store name, brand accent colour, page layout and default view. Choose a full-window layout or show the store inside your theme.
6. Preview the store while logged in. When it is ready, publish the page and enable **Open the virtual store to shoppers**.

To help customers find the store, add its page to your menu, use the `[aisleza_link]` shortcode or enable the optional **Enter the virtual store** banner above your shop grid. Aisleza never changes your menus automatically.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= What is a 3D virtual store? =

A 3D virtual store (or virtual showroom) is a shop customers walk through on your website, like visiting a real store: aisles, shelves and a trolley, all in the browser. Aisleza builds one from your WooCommerce categories and products, so it stays in sync with your catalog. Customers do not need a VR headset or an app.

= How is a virtual store different from a 3D product viewer? =

A 3D product viewer lets customers rotate one product on its product page. A virtual store shows your whole catalog as a space to walk through, so customers browse many products, move between aisles and add items to the trolley as they go. You can use both: Aisleza Pro can also show 3D product models on the shelves.

= Is Aisleza free? =

Yes. The free plugin is the full walk-in 3D store, with no time limit and on as many sites as you like. Aisleza Pro is an optional paid add-on for extra interiors, merchandising, a shopping assistant and shopper insights.

= Does Aisleza replace my existing shop or checkout? =

No. The virtual store is an additional page on your website. Your regular shop, product pages and checkout stay available, and the virtual trolley uses the same WooCommerce cart.

= Do I need 3D models of my products? =

No. The free plugin creates shelf displays from your existing product images. Showing your own 3D product models is an optional Pro feature.

= Can I display the store within my theme? =

Yes. Choose **Inside my theme** to show the store within your page content, with your theme's header and footer. Choose **Full window** for a dedicated store view with a link back to your regular shop.

= How does Aisleza handle loading and performance? =

The virtual store's scripts load only on the store page, and the 3D engine loads when a customer enters the store. Nearby aisles load as the customer moves through it, and nothing animates while the customer stands still. Performance depends on the device, browser, product images and store setup. Customers can also use the lighter shelf wall or list view.

= Which product types can customers buy inside the store? =

Simple and variable products can be added straight to the trolley. WooCommerce stock limits, backorder settings and sold-individually restrictions apply. Grouped, external and other special product types link to their product pages.

= Can customers use it on a phone? =

Yes. The mobile experience includes an on-screen joystick and a lighter 3D view. Customers can switch to the shelf wall or list view if they prefer.

= Does Aisleza store personal data or set cookies? =

The free plugin does not collect personal data or set its own cookies. It uses browser session storage to remember display preferences and navigation state, such as the last aisle and trolley position, which is cleared when the tab closes. The cart uses WooCommerce's own session.

Optional add-ons and integrations may process additional data. Review their documentation and settings when you enable them.

= Does Aisleza connect to external services? =

No, not the free plugin's core store. It runs on your own website, its 3D engine is bundled with the plugin, and product data comes from your WooCommerce store.

Optional integrations and add-on features, such as AI conversations or externally hosted videos, may connect to external services when you enable them.

= Can I use full-page caching on the store page? =

Yes. The store page's initial content contains no cart data or security tokens, so it works with full-page caching. After setting up your cache, check that adding products, changing quantities and going to checkout work as expected.

= Does Aisleza work with Shopify, Wix or other platforms? =

This plugin is for WooCommerce. Aisleza is also available for Shopify, Wix, BigCommerce, Magento, PrestaShop, OpenCart, Shopware and Squarespace. See [aisleza.com](https://aisleza.com/extensions/) for each platform.

= What happens when I delete the plugin? =

Aisleza removes its settings. The page you created for the virtual store stays in WordPress, so you can edit or delete it yourself.

== Changelog ==

= 1.1.3 =
* Added free shipping progress in the trolley: "Add $X more for free shipping" with a progress bar, when the customer's shipping zone has free shipping above a minimum order amount. It follows WooCommerce's own free shipping rule.
* Added star ratings and review counts in the product window (hidden for products without reviews, or when star ratings are turned off in WooCommerce).
* Added "You may also like" in the trolley, from your products' cross-sells. Choosing one walks the customer to its shelf and opens it.
* Added the Aisleza 3D Store block for the block editor. New store pages use it; the `[aisleza]` shortcode keeps working.
* Added an "Upgrade To Premium" link on the Plugins screen and in the Aisleza menu (hidden when Aisleza Pro is active).

= 1.1.2 =
* Added saved products: a heart in the product window saves a product for later on the shopper's device (no account), "Saved products" in the store's drawer lists them, saved products show a heart on their shelf boxes, and "Share my list" gives a link that opens the list in the store.
* Added lite mode: older phones and slow computers automatically get a lighter 3D store (smaller drawing size, fewer plants, photo boxes instead of 3D models); a store that still draws slowly switches to lite while the shopper walks and remembers it on that device. No setting needed.
* Fixed: entering the store keeps the whole store in view on themes where the page jumped and cut off its top.

= 1.1.1 =
* Added links that open the 3D store at a product or an aisle: a product link walks the shopper to the product and opens it, an aisle link walks to that aisle.
* Added "Share this product" in the product window (the phone's share sheet, or a copied link).
* Added "Show me where it is" for search results in the walk-in store: it walks the shopper to the product's shelf.
* Added an optional "See it in the 3D store" button for product pages (Settings, Ways in; off by default).
* Added "New" and "Only N left" stickers on the shelves, from the product's date added (30 days) and WooCommerce's own low-stock count.
* Added extension points for add-ons: a product window event, opening an open store at a product or aisle, and stores reaching add-ons that load after the store opened.

= 1.1.0 =
* Added shopper events that add-ons can use to track store entry, aisle visits, product views, cart additions and checkout navigation. The core plugin does not send these events anywhere; add-ons may process or send them.
* Added support for add-ons to show GLB (.glb) 3D product models on shelves in place of image-based displays.
* Both additions are optional and leave the existing store experience unchanged when no add-on uses them.

= 1.0.0 =

* Browse products, select variations and add items to the existing WooCommerce cart.
* Reach WooCommerce checkout through the trolley or checkout lanes.
